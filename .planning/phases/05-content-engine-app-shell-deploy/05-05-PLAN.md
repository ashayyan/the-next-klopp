**As a** learner, **I want to** have lessons authored as validated data that render in a navigable app protected on Vercel, **so that** every shipped lesson is traceable, tested, never public and never broken.

<objective>
Turn the minimal lesson page into the DESIGN.md section 6 lesson frame from 05-UI-SPEC: regions, the eight-stage to seven-rail mapping, the step nav with its decided CTA copy, deep links, focus and announcements, the complete view and the per-template layout at 375, 768 and 1440.

Implements UI-SPEC "Lesson runtime /learn/:lessonId" (regions, rail mapping table, step, visual, nav, layout per width, column spans by template, text-only and question screens, screen change behaviour, lesson complete), UI-SPEC "Accessibility contract" (focus to `h1` on route change and to `h2` on screen change, one polite region), UI Considerations rows "Lesson frame", "Stage mapping", "Step CTA copy", "Deep link", "Lesson complete". CONTEXT "Navigation: /learn/:lessonId (lesson runtime with step frame)". Loading, error and unknown-id states follow in 05-07; claims fill the source region in 05-09; interactions fill the visual region in 05-11.

Planner decision: `StepRail` gains an additive `allComplete?: boolean` prop for the complete view; `ShellBar`'s `focus`, `onFocusChange` and `confusion` become optional (Phase 6 adds them back as controls); `LESSON_LOOP_STEPS` is unchanged (design baselines depend on it).

Purpose: a learner moves through any lesson with one obvious action per screen, with keyboard and screen reader.
Output: frame, nav, complete view, layout table, breadcrumb, page-title hook; walker asserts CTA copy and the complete view.
</objective>
