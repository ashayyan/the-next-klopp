**As a** learner, **I want to** have lessons authored as validated data that render in a navigable app protected on Vercel, **so that** every shipped lesson is traceable, tested, never public and never broken.

<objective>
Add the teaching-shape rules of CONT-02: the 60-word screen, the Lesson Loop order, prerequisite cycles, known ids for tracks, chapters, concepts, roles and competencies, duration, heading length and the unbuilt-template gate, each with its UI-SPEC message and a failing fixture.

Implements CONTEXT "Validator (CONT-02)" rules `screen-words`, `loop-stage`, `prereq-cycle` and the structural rules "unknown role/competency/concept id, duration 5–12", UI-SPEC "Typography" (step heading at most 8 words), UI-SPEC "Column spans by template" (`template-unbuilt`), post-research decision "Chapter ids are part-a, part-b… from the concept-map part field" (the validator checks every concept in a lesson has that track and part, RESEARCH Open Question 2). QA-04's "unknown role or competency fails validation" lands here.

Rules apply to every lesson of any status except `template-unbuilt`, which applies to `shippable` and `shipped` only.

Purpose: a lesson that is too long, out of order, circular or tagged with unknown ids cannot ship.
Output: five rule modules, a cycle finder, nine fixture folders.
</objective>
