**As a** learner, **I want to** have lessons authored as validated data that render in a navigable app protected on Vercel, **so that** every shipped lesson is traceable, tested, never public and never broken.

<objective>
Make "shippable" mean "checked by someone else against the sources": the validator refuses a shippable lesson without a fresh, complete verification record from a verifier other than its author (Opus for rules and stats) and without role and competency tags; a helper lets the separate verifier write that record without ever storing copyrighted text.

Implements CONTEXT "Shippable gate location?" (same validator; verification.json plus non-empty roles and competencies), "Verification record format?" (fields, verifier ≠ author, Opus for metrics and regulations, stale hash, unsupported claim), "Who verifies in this run?" (the record is written by a separate sub-agent; this plan builds the gate and the helper, 05-18 runs the verifier), "What's new source?" (the validator requires an entry when a lesson flips to shipped). RESEARCH Pattern 7 (claim ids, hash excludes status, excerptSha256 reuse, excerptId link, 25-word paraphrase), Pitfall 5. QA-04's "lesson cannot be shippable without role and competency tags" lands here.

Purpose: QA-01, the verification path is enforced in code, not trust.
Output: hash, two rules plus excerpt-length, the verifier helper, eleven fixture folders.
</objective>
