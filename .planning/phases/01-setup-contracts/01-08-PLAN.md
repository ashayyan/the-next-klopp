## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-01-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | `@agentmemory/agentmemory` and `@agentmemory/mcp` pinned to 0.9.33 (the verified release); no new project dependencies |
