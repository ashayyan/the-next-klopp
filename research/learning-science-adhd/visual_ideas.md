# Learning-science mechanics for the Lesson Loop

## Predict, then reveal (Loop step: Show)

Before each pitch animation the learner drags a marker to where they think the pass goes, then the real answer plays over their guess. A wrong guess still counts as study. [src-richland-pretesting-2009]

## Worked example then fade (Loop step: Show to Touch)

The first lesson on a topic walks through a solved board with labels beside the visuals. The next one hides one step, then two, so the learner takes over gradually. [src-barbieri-worked-examples-2023][src-noetel-multimedia-design-2021]

## Touch the board (Loop step: Touch)

Learners drag players on a pitch and see the shape change. Novices get scaffolds such as ghost positions that switch off as they improve. [src-chernikova-simulation-learning-2020][src-freeman-active-learning-2014]

## Small-card recall set (Loop step: Practise)

A low-energy card set of a handful of recall questions that counts as a full day. Cards are due by schedule and capped, with no overdue counter. [src-cepeda-distributed-practice-2006][src-agarwal-retrieval-classroom-2021]

## Self-test nudge (Loop step: Practise)

After a lesson the app asks the learner to cover the screen and recall, then shows the answer. Adults with ADHD tend to skip self-testing though it helps them. [src-knouse-adhd-self-testing-2011][src-minear-adhd-retrieval-2023]

## Mastery ring with no streak (Loop step: Reflect)

A ring fills with concepts recalled correctly after a gap. It never resets or shows a lost streak, and it gives competence feedback instead of points. [src-deci-extrinsic-rewards-1999][src-nber-streaks-2025]

## Start-plan prompt (Loop step: Start)

One optional line: when I sit down, I will open this card set. The learner writes it once and the app shows it on low-motivation days. [src-gollwitzer-implementation-intentions-2006]
