# Learning science for an adult ADHD visual learner: findings

Scope: learning design only, not treatment. Evidence grades sit in each source's notes in sources.json. Most learning-science studies below did not test people with ADHD, so those claims are labelled as general evidence applied to ADHD by extrapolation.

## Key concepts

### General learning science (extrapolated to ADHD, not tested there)

- Retrieval practice means trying to recall something instead of rereading it, and a review of 50 classroom experiments found benefits across ages, subjects and test types. [src-agarwal-retrieval-classroom-2021]
- Retrieving soon after learning slows forgetting, and the benefit appears for many materials and learner abilities. [src-mcdermott-practicing-retrieval-2020]
- Spacing study sessions apart beats massing them, and the best gap grows as the needed retention time grows. [src-cepeda-distributed-practice-2006]
- An expert monograph rated practice testing and distributed practice as the two high-utility techniques among ten, and rereading and highlighting as low. [src-dunlosky-learning-techniques-2013]
- Dual coding means pairing words with relevant visuals, and a tutorial review lists it among six strategies with strong support. [src-weinstein-six-strategies-2018]
- A review of 29 meta-analyses found positive learning effects for signaling, spatial and temporal contiguity, segmenting, coherence and modality principles. [src-noetel-multimedia-design-2021]
- Worked examples (a solved problem to study before solving) show a medium average effect on maths performance. [src-barbieri-worked-examples-2023]
- Simulation-based learning shows a large overall effect (g 0.85) in higher education, and learners with low prior knowledge benefit when given examples. [src-chernikova-simulation-learning-2020]
- Active learning raised exam performance by about 0.47 SD over lecturing in undergraduate STEM. [src-freeman-active-learning-2014]
- Answering a question before studying, even wrongly, improved later learning versus extra study time in five experiments. [src-richland-pretesting-2009]
- Gamification has small positive effects on cognitive (g .49), motivational (g .36) and behavioural (g .25) outcomes, and the last two were less stable in rigorous studies. [src-sailer-gamification-meta-2019]
- Expected tangible rewards reduced intrinsic motivation in a meta-analysis of 128 experiments, while positive feedback increased it. [src-deci-extrinsic-rewards-1999]
- If-then plans ("when X happens I will do Y") gave a medium-to-large boost to goal attainment across many goals. [src-gollwitzer-implementation-intentions-2006]

### ADHD-specific (studies that sampled people with ADHD)

- Inattention often persists into young adulthood, and inattention is more tied to academic problems than hyperactivity is. [src-faraone-wfadhd-consensus-2021]
- Meta-analyses of ADHD show a moderate tendency to choose small immediate rewards over larger delayed ones. [src-faraone-wfadhd-consensus-2021]
- Adults with ADHD score lower on verbal long-term memory, and the deficit tracks poor encoding, not retrieval. [src-skodzik-adult-adhd-memory-2016]
- Adults with ADHD predicted their recall as accurately as controls but used self-testing less, and self-testing explained much of the group difference. [src-knouse-adhd-self-testing-2011]
- College students with ADHD, especially with inattention, tend to have lower academic performance in mostly observational studies. [src-pagespetit-adhd-college-2024]
- Models of altered reward sensitivity in ADHD disagree, and the experimental evidence for them is inconsistent. [src-luman-adhd-reinforcement-2010]

## Must teach

### General learning science (design rules for the Lesson Loop)

- Make every lesson end with recall from memory instead of rereading, and re-test on later days. [src-agarwal-retrieval-classroom-2021][src-dunlosky-learning-techniques-2013]
- Space reviews with a gap that grows with the delay you want to survive, so schedule by due date and not by marathon. [src-cepeda-distributed-practice-2006]
- Ask a prediction before the reveal, since an unsuccessful guess still helps later learning. [src-richland-pretesting-2009]
- Put words next to the visual they describe, signal the key part, and split content into small segments. [src-noetel-multimedia-design-2021]
- Show a solved example before asking the learner to solve one, especially early in a topic. [src-barbieri-worked-examples-2023][src-chernikova-simulation-learning-2020]
- Let the learner manipulate the pitch board or simulation, and add scaffolds for novices. [src-chernikova-simulation-learning-2020][src-freeman-active-learning-2014]
- Reward with feedback about competence and progress, not tangible points that can crowd out interest. [src-deci-extrinsic-rewards-1999][src-sailer-gamification-meta-2019]
- On a low-motivation day, let the learner pre-write one if-then plan for starting (for example, after coffee, open one card set). [src-gollwitzer-implementation-intentions-2006]
- Treat sessions of any length as valid, because lecture-length rules of thumb have thin support. [src-bradbury-attention-span-2016]

### ADHD-specific (what the ADHD data justify)

- Build retrieval into the product, since it helped college students with ADHD as much as controls in one small study. [src-minear-adhd-retrieval-2023]
- Support encoding with a worked example or a prompt for a deeper strategy first, because unmedicated participants used fewer deep strategies and still scored lower. [src-minear-adhd-retrieval-2023][src-skodzik-adult-adhd-memory-2016]
- Prompt self-testing because adults with ADHD under-use it even though it helps them. [src-knouse-adhd-self-testing-2011]
- Give quick, near-term feedback inside a lesson, since delay discounting is moderately stronger in ADHD. [src-faraone-wfadhd-consensus-2021]
- Do not market the app as attention training, since transfer from computer training to academic skills is not supported in children and is small in adults. [src-rapport-adhd-cognitive-training-2013][src-cortese-adhd-cognitive-training-2015][src-elbe-adult-adhd-cognitive-training-2023]
- Offer if-then planning as a low-risk optional prompt, as the ADHD evidence is only from two small child studies. [src-gawrilow-adhd-if-then-2011]

## Misconceptions

### General learning science

- "People stop paying attention after a fixed ten-to-fifteen-minute mark" traces back to one weakly relevant source and has no strong support. [src-bradbury-attention-span-2016]
### ADHD-specific

- "Brain-training games rebuild focus that carries into study" is unsupported in children with ADHD and the adult evidence shows small cognitive gain with no clear symptom or working-memory change. [src-rapport-adhd-cognitive-training-2013][src-elbe-adult-adhd-cognitive-training-2023]
### General learning science

- "Rereading and highlighting are good enough" misses that they rated low against practice testing and spacing. [src-dunlosky-learning-techniques-2013]
### ADHD-specific

- "Learners with ADHD need a different method altogether" is not what the retrieval data show, since the testing benefit was present in the group. [src-minear-adhd-retrieval-2023]
### General learning science

- "Points always raise motivation" ignores that expected tangible rewards lowered intrinsic interest in the meta-analysis. [src-deci-extrinsic-rewards-1999]

## Contested points

Contested: Streaks. One side says highlighting streaks raised platform use and maths scores in a randomised trial with children, so a streak display is a motivator. The other side says expected rewards can undercut intrinsic interest, and reward-sensitivity findings in ADHD are inconsistent, so loss-framed streaks may hurt. This project avoids lost streaks until adult evidence exists. [src-nber-streaks-2025][src-deci-extrinsic-rewards-1999][src-luman-adhd-reinforcement-2010]

Contested: Gamification overall. Pooled results show small gains in learning, yet the motivational and behavioural gains weakened in higher-quality studies, and the same review notes the drivers are unresolved. [src-sailer-gamification-meta-2019][src-deci-extrinsic-rewards-1999]

Contested: Cognitive training. Unblinded raters reported larger benefits than blinded raters in children with ADHD, so some of the claimed benefit may come from expectation, while a small adult meta-analysis found a slight gain in overall cognition and no symptom change. [src-cortese-adhd-cognitive-training-2015][src-elbe-adult-adhd-cognitive-training-2023]

## Open questions

- No adult-ADHD trial of spaced repetition queues, caps or a welcome-back flow was found, so these rest on general spacing evidence.
- No ADHD sample was found for the multimedia, worked-example or simulation findings above.
- Does a daily cap or a low-energy micro-session count as a full day for retention? The microlearning literature was not appraised.
- Does self-testing in ADHD hold up with football content and longer delays than word pairs?
