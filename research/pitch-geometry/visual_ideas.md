# Pitch geometry: visual ideas

## Legal range slider (Touch)

The learner drags the touchline handle and the goal-line handle on one outline pitch. The legal zone is shaded, the international zone sits inside it, and the handle snaps to a red state outside the range. A badge turns on when the touchline is shorter than the goal line, because the Law forbids that. The canonical 105 x 68 m marker stays pinned so the learner sees a convention sitting inside the Law range. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#law1-touchline-length][src-ifab-laws-2026-27#law1-goal-line-length][src-ifab-laws-2026-27#law1-touchline-longer]

## Marking builder (Show, then Touch)

Markings draw on one at a time in Law order: halfway line, centre circle, goal area, penalty area, mark, arc, corner area. Each step shows its distance as a dimension line from the inside of the goalpost, and a toggle swaps the dimension lines between metres and yards. A peel-back button shows where the line edge sits, so lines that belong to the area are visible. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#law1-penalty-area][src-ifab-laws-2026-27#law1-measure-outside]

## Spot the D (Practise)

The learner taps where the penalty area ends and where the arc begins on a zoomed penalty box. The arc fills only outside the box, which fixes the "D is part of the area" misconception through the correction rather than a warning. Three taps, instant feedback, then one fresh variant. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#law1-penalty-arc]

## Two-pitch overlay (Show, then Touch)

Two registered Premier League pitches are drawn at true scale on shared goal lines and a shared centre, and the learner drags a ghost of the second over the first. Fulham against a 105 x 68 m club is the default pair, with a one-tap swap to Liverpool or Chelsea. A caption states these are registered figures, not surveyed ones. [src-pl-handbook-2026-27#club-fulham-pitch][src-pl-handbook-2026-27#club-chelsea-pitch][src-pl-handbook-2026-27#clubs-standard-105x68]

## Same coordinate, different stadium (Touch, then Practise)

One shot at coordinate (110, 40) in the StatsBomb grid is plotted on a 105 x 68 m pitch and on a 100 x 65 m pitch. The learner measures the distance to goal on both and sees the gap. Then they pick which pitch a Wyscout event came from, using the fixed penalty area as the clue. [src-statsbomb-open-data-spec#location-centre][src-mplsoccer-standardize-docs#fixed-penalty-area][src-pl-handbook-2026-27#club-fulham-pitch]

## Provider frame flipper (Show, then Practise)

One pass is drawn on a single pitch while a switch re-expresses it in StatsBomb, Opta, Wyscout, kloppy and tracking frames. Origin dot, axis arrows and the range labels animate between frames. A quiz hides the labels and asks the learner to say which frame a pair of numbers belongs to. [src-kloppy-coordinate-systems-source#statsbomb-system][src-kloppy-coordinate-systems-source#opta-system][src-kloppy-coordinate-systems-source#tracab-system][src-wyscout-data-glossary-pitch-coordinates#x-attack-100]

## Grass stripes layer (Show)

A subtle mowing-stripe layer can be toggled on the canonical pitch, with stripes running across the width. It is decoration only and sits under every data mark, so it never competes with a heatmap. [src-pl-handbook-2026-27#rule-k22-mowing]
