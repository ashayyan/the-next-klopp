# Tactics history: visual ideas

## Scrubbable timeline with a morphing shape
Lesson Loop step: Touch it. A timeline runs from 1863 to 2019. The learner drags a handle and eleven dots on a pitch glide between the 2-3-5, WM, 4-2-4, 4-4-2 and 4-3-3. An offside-line marker on the pitch jumps from three defenders to two at 1925, and the IFAB note appears. [src-ifab-history-offside-law#offside-1925-three-to-two] [src-wikipedia-formation-association-football]

## Guess the decade
Lesson Loop step: Predict, then reveal. Show only a shape and one line of context, for example a deep centre-forward dragging a centre-half out. The learner picks a decade, then the reveal names the match and the rule or coach behind it. [src-uefa-hungary-1950-56] [src-arsenal-newcastle-7-0-golden-era]

## Offside lab: three or two
Lesson Loop step: Touch it, then Do it. A slider swaps the pre-1925 and post-1925 rule. The learner drags a forward and watches who is onside, then builds a back line that catches him offside under each rule. [src-ifab-history-offside-law#offside-1990-level-is-onside] [src-ifab-history-back-pass-law#backpass-1992-introduced]

## Two-sided credit map
Lesson Loop step: Connect it. Branching lines show who is credited with pressing, catenaccio and the WM. Each claim is a node tagged Contested, with a toggle to switch between the rival accounts and their sources. [src-fourfourtwo-who-invented-pressing] [src-premier-league-counter-pressing-explained] [src-wikipedia-catenaccio] [src-90min-nereo-rocco] [src-aisa-chapman-new-formation-1925] [src-whiterose-evolution-football-passing] [src-thesefootballtimes-roots-of-xg] [src-theanalyst-what-is-xg]

## Premier League card ladder
Lesson Loop step: Do it. Seven cards, one per Premier League anchor from 1992 to 2019, each with a tiny pitch shape. The learner orders them, then each reveal links to the club or league source. [src-premier-league-history] [src-premier-league-hall-of-fame-wenger] [src-premier-league-chelsea-2004-05] [src-premier-league-leicester-2015-16] [src-premier-league-man-city-evolution] [src-premier-league-klopp-liverpool-2019-20] [src-bundesliga-klopp-dortmund-years]

## Possession guess
Lesson Loop step: Predict, then reveal. The learner drags a marker to guess Leicester's average possession as champions, then the bar snaps to the real figure beside other winners. [src-premier-league-leicester-2015-16#lowest-possession-champion]

## Press lineage and pitch compactness
Lesson Loop step: See it. Two pitch panels show man-marking with a sweeper beside Sacchi-style compact lines, with Total Football's pressing as the bridge. [src-uefa-ac-milan-1988-90] [src-uefa-ajax-1971-73] [src-uefa-barcelona-2008-12]

## Lock it in cards
Lesson Loop step: Lock it in. Each anchor becomes a retrieval card: law, coach, shape and why it mattered. Cards feed the spaced queue. [src-ifab-history-origins-modern-game#fa-1863-first-laws] [src-fourfourtwo-england-1966-formation]
