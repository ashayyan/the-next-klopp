# Role definitions: findings

## Key concepts

- Titles are unstable: sporting director, director of football, technical director and head of football describe overlapping jobs, and clubs mix them. [src-pl-sporting-directors-explained][src-jobsinfootball-sporting-director-role]
- Academic work calls the sporting director role conceptually and operationally misunderstood, which is why one club's job description cannot define it. [src-parnell-sporting-director-emergence]
- The Premier League has moved from the all-powerful manager to a head coach inside a structure that splits scouting, recruitment, medical and data. [src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager]
- Most public job ads give duties and skills but no reporting line and no KPIs, so those fields below are flagged as inferred or "not stated". [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist][src-liverpool-set-piece-coach]
- Competency phrases for synthesis: builds opposition reports, ranks targets with models, negotiates contracts and loans, owns the data platform, leads a department and translates between coach and specialists. [src-brentford-head-of-analysis-2021][src-newcastle-lead-recruitment-data-analyst][src-liverpool-data-engineer]

## Must teach

### sporting-director

- Purpose: overall responsibility for football operations, aligning academy and first team so one playing style runs through the club. [src-pl-sporting-directors-explained]
- Responsibilities: sets sporting strategy, appoints the head coach and staff, builds scouting, analytics and video, runs squad renewal and contracts. [src-jobsinfootball-sporting-director-role][src-pl-sporting-directors-explained]
- Reports to the owner, chairperson or chief executive; the head coach usually sits below. [src-pl-sporting-directors-explained]
- Decisions owned: usually the final call on recruitment, the head coach hire and the long-term plan. [src-pl-sporting-directors-explained][src-slemen-effective-sporting-director]
- KPIs: no formal list; judged mainly on transfer record and long-term building, and fails through knee-jerk coach sackings, vague vision or a watered-down remit. [src-pl-sporting-directors-explained][src-slemen-effective-sporting-director]
- Entry: no single route; coach, scout and analytics paths all take many years, with business and leadership courses rather than one licence. [src-jobsinfootball-sporting-director-role]

### head-coach

- Purpose: lead first-team performance inside a club structure that shares recruitment, medical and data with others. [src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager]
- Responsibilities: player and team development, technical philosophy, high-performance culture and seasonal planning. [src-uefa-coaching-licences]
- Reports to the sporting director at most clubs, where the old manager had everyone reporting to him. [src-pl-sporting-directors-explained][src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager]
- Decisions owned: selection, tactics and training; a signing rarely happens without the head coach involved, but who leads recruitment varies. [src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager]
- KPIs: no published list; the cited failure mode is public conflict with the board over support, which preceded two dismissals. [src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager]
- Entry: UEFA A Licence, then the Pro Diploma of 360 hours, which also needs a year of coaching after the A. [src-uefa-coaching-licences]

### head-of-performance-analysis

- Purpose: lead the analysis department so coaches get opposition work and live in-game input. [src-brentford-head-of-analysis-2021]
- Responsibilities: manage people and process, present opposition analysis, assess team and individual performance against the playing style. [src-brentford-head-of-analysis-2021]
- Reports to: not stated; the post serves the sporting director and head coaches and sits beside a head of data and insights. [src-newcastle-head-recruitment-analysis-women]
- Decisions owned (inferred): department process and the content of opposition packs. [src-brentford-head-of-analysis-2021]
- KPIs: none stated; ads ask for management experience in an elite department and presenting insight to stakeholders. [src-newcastle-head-recruitment-analysis-women]
- Entry: elite analysis experience, a sports degree, Sportscode skill, with UEFA B and data-platform experience desirable. [src-newcastle-first-team-analyst-post-match]

### head-of-data-science

- Purpose: advance club understanding through statistical modelling for match analysis, player performance, youth development and talent identification. [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist]
- Responsibilities: build and test models on event and tracking data, deploy and monitor them, run self-service platforms. [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist]
- Reports to: not stated; works with women's, men's and academy staff and with recruitment. [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist][src-newcastle-lead-recruitment-data-analyst]
- Decisions owned (inferred): which models reach production and how accuracy is judged. [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist]
- KPIs: none stated; the ad asks for quantified uncertainty and quick, clear communication to varied audiences. [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist]
- Entry: STEM Master's or PhD, several years in data science, football analytics experience, Python or R. [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist]

### head-of-recruitment

- Purpose: lead player identification and signing, combining scouting with analytics that fit the playing style. [src-liverpool-women-head-of-recruitment]
- Responsibilities: strategy, squad planning, contract structures, agent and club relationships, loan networks. [src-liverpool-women-head-of-recruitment]
- Reports to: no line manager stated; works with the managing director, data science and coaches. [src-liverpool-women-head-of-recruitment]
- Decisions owned: shortlists and recommendations; the final signing call often sits with a director, manager or owner. [src-football365-who-controls-transfers]
- KPIs: recommendations backed by data, reports and footage, trusted by coaches, and fitting style and character. [src-hudl-marshall-head-of-recruitment]
- Entry: credible first-team or academy football background and a record of identifying talent. [src-liverpool-women-head-of-recruitment]

### academy-director

- Purpose: run an academy that is successful, sustainable and focused on developing young talent. [src-newcastle-women-academy-manager]
- Responsibilities: staff, logistics, compliance, education, pathways, recruitment with the club, safeguarding. [src-newcastle-women-academy-manager]
- Reports to: not stated; the only evidence is a women's academy manager ad, so men's Premier League academy reporting is unevidenced. [src-newcastle-women-academy-manager]
- Decisions owned: player pathways and programme compliance with governing-body rules. [src-newcastle-women-academy-manager]
- KPIs: none stated; the measurable duties are regulatory compliance and welfare standards. [src-newcastle-women-academy-manager]
- Entry: related degree or UEFA B Licence plus substantial academy experience. [src-newcastle-women-academy-manager]

### club-ceo

- Purpose: manage finances, commercial growth and sporting ambition, turning the owners' vision into daily operations. [src-footballbenchmark-ceo-profiles]
- Responsibilities: club-wide strategy across football and business departments. [src-footballbenchmark-ceo-profiles]
- Reports to: the owners (inferred); in a CEO-led model the sporting director is one department head under the CEO. [src-footballbenchmark-ceo-profiles][src-jobsinfootball-sporting-director-structure]
- Decisions owned: budgets and club structure. [src-jobsinfootball-sporting-director-structure]
- KPIs: no list; CEOs stay in post longer than sporting directors, and Premier League CEO tenure is the shortest of the big five leagues. [src-footballbenchmark-ceo-profiles]
- Entry: commercial and media careers are most common, then finance and football roles, via internal promotion or external hire. [src-footballbenchmark-ceo-profiles]

### head-of-performance

- Purpose: oversee sports science, medicine, conditioning, nutrition and analytics so as many players as possible are fit. [src-slemen-director-high-performance]
- Responsibilities: lead the performance staff and translate between head coach and specialists. [src-slemen-director-high-performance]
- Reports to: varies by club; an academy ad links the lead to a director of medical and performance. [src-liverpool-head-academy-medical-performance]
- Decisions owned: injury prevention, return to play, workload management and medical input to selection. [src-liverpool-head-academy-medical-performance]
- KPIs: player availability; the role struggles in clubs where recruitment and agents dominate. [src-liverpool-head-academy-medical-performance][src-slemen-director-high-performance]
- Entry: sports science or conditioning route, or a medical route needing GMC registration. [src-liverpool-head-academy-medical-performance]

### assistant-coach

- Runs training day to day, leads the other coaches, filters issues for the manager and backs his message on matchday. [src-pl-assistant-manager-insights]

### set-piece-coach

- Analyses opponents' set pieces, designs plans, coaches routines, agrees training time with the head coach; UEFA A or equivalent. [src-liverpool-set-piece-coach]

### loan-manager

- Tracks loanees through matches and footage, vets host clubs and supports welfare off the pitch. [src-sky-hammond-loans-manager]
- At a top club it also leads placements, contracts and negotiations. [src-liverpool-head-of-loan-management]

### chief-scout

- Thin evidence: no chief scout ad was read; the role sits within a scouting network led from recruitment. [src-hudl-marshall-head-of-recruitment][src-football365-who-controls-transfers]

### first-team-analyst

- Covers pre-match, live and post-match analysis, training footage and individual development plans. [src-liverpool-first-team-performance-analyst][src-newcastle-first-team-analyst-post-match]

### recruitment-analyst

- Ranks targets with data models, builds dashboards and presents recommendations to stakeholders. [src-newcastle-lead-recruitment-data-analyst]

### data-engineer

- Builds pipelines and the warehouse, supports model deployment and handles large tracking data, in Python and SQL. [src-liverpool-data-engineer]

## Misconceptions

- "The manager signs the players at every club": recruitment ranges from manager-led to director-led, and ownership can hold the final say. [src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager][src-football365-who-controls-transfers]
- "Sporting director and director of football are different jobs": the Premier League treats the terms as often interchangeable. [src-pl-sporting-directors-explained]

## Contested points

Contested: Where the sporting director ends and the head coach begins. One view gives the director recruitment and the long-term plan so the coach focuses on the next match [src-slemen-effective-sporting-director][src-pl-sporting-directors-explained]; the other holds that coaches resist losing recruitment control, as at clubs where the lines stayed unclear. [src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager]

Contested: Whether the sporting director belongs under the CEO or on the board. Supporters of the CEO-led model cite simple lines; interviewees favouring a board seat say the director cannot argue football's case without one. [src-jobsinfootball-sporting-director-structure][src-slemen-effective-sporting-director]

Contested: Whether data leads or supports recruitment. One recruitment data analyst ad has data models ranking targets, while a head of recruitment described data as a supporting analyst beside scouts. [src-newcastle-lead-recruitment-data-analyst][src-hudl-marshall-head-of-recruitment]
