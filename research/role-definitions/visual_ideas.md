# Role definitions: visual ideas for the Role Map

## Role card with a reporting line (Lesson Loop: Show)

Each role opens as a card: purpose, duties, who they report to, decisions owned. A small org chart sits beside it and can flip between a CEO-led and a board-level layout, so the learner sees the sporting director move. [src-jobsinfootball-sporting-director-structure] Fields the ads never state show a dashed outline labelled "inferred" instead of invented text. [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist]

## Who decides this signing? (Lesson Loop: Touch)

A transfer pipeline runs left to right: scout, recruitment analyst, head of recruitment, sporting director, CEO, owner. The learner drags the final-say token between stops and watches which roles light up. Presets show a manager-led, director-led and owner-led club. [src-football365-who-controls-transfers][src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager]

## Week in the life (Lesson Loop: Show)

An animated timeline for a match week: an analyst films training, builds the opposition pack, and joins live analysis on matchday. [src-liverpool-first-team-performance-analyst] A second track follows a set-piece coach agreeing training time with the head coach. [src-liverpool-set-piece-coach] A transfer-window track follows a loans manager monitoring loanees. [src-sky-hammond-loans-manager]

## Coach licence ladder (Lesson Loop: Practise)

A stair-step graphic climbs from C to Pro with the hours and entry rule at each step. The learner places a fictional coach's history on the ladder and the board says whether they qualify for the Pro course. [src-uefa-coaching-licences]

## Role path through the concept map (Lesson Loop: Hook)

Pick a role and the concept map lights its required chapters, with competencies written as phrases such as "ranks targets with models" or "owns the data platform". [src-newcastle-lead-recruitment-data-analyst][src-liverpool-data-engineer] A readiness ring fills as the learner clears each competency level.

## Failure cards (Lesson Loop: Practise)

Short scenarios where the sporting director sacks a coach after a bad run, or a head coach criticises the board in public. The learner picks the better move and sees how the cited sources judge it. [src-slemen-effective-sporting-director][src-espn-head-coach-vs-manager]

## Career entry routes (Lesson Loop: Show)

Three parallel tracks (coach, scout, analytics) climb toward the sporting director, with the CEO route from commercial and finance drawn separately. [src-jobsinfootball-sporting-director-role][src-footballbenchmark-ceo-profiles] The learner traces where a data-science graduate enters. [src-liverpool-lead-data-scientist]
