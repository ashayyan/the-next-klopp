# Interactive explainers: mechanics for the Lesson Loop

## Credit-split slider (Lesson Loop: Predict, then reveal)

Show one real Premier League build-up and give the learner a slider to split credit between two passes. Only after they commit does the xT value change appear. Keep the guess and the answer on the same pitch. [src-karun-expected-threat]

## Value surface scrubber (Lesson Loop: Touch it)

A single slider steps a zone-value grid from shot quality alone to deeper look-ahead. Hover or tab to a zone to read its value. A label states what each step adds, one idea per step. [src-karun-expected-threat][src-setosa-markov-chains]

## Editable move-or-shoot table (Lesson Loop: Do it)

The learner edits the shoot and move shares for a few zones. A bad row turns red with a short message, so the rule that shares sum to one is learned from feedback. [src-setosa-markov-chains]

## Play first, name it second (Lesson Loop: See it, then Touch it)

For pitch control, let the learner drag one defender and watch the shaded zones shift before any term appears. Then name the idea in one sentence. [src-ncase-polygons][src-soccermatics-pitch-control]

## Layer-by-layer pitch (Lesson Loop: See it)

Start with two players and add one layer per tap: the ball, the passing lines, the Voronoi regions. Earlier controls stay live. A second viewpoint toggles between pitch plan and the player's eye. [src-ciechanowski-moon]

## Stepper for processes (Lesson Loop: See it)

Pressing triggers, build-up phases and pass chains each get a manual next and back control with numbered steps, never an auto-play loop. [src-redblobgames-astar]

## Tour with an explorer door (Lesson Loop: Connect it)

Every chapter ends with a guided tour card and a search box over the same data, so the learner can wander once they have the path. [src-iib-futbolismo-legends]

## Spacing guess (Lesson Loop: Lock it in)

Before the review queue is explained, ask which gap pattern keeps a fact longest, then simulate it. The learner sees why the queue works. [src-ncase-remember]

## Colour-safe team marks (Lesson Loop: See it)

Teams use Okabe-Ito blue and vermilion plus a ring or number shape, so colour is never the only cue. Contrast checks follow the 4.5 to 1 rule for text. [src-okabe-ito-cud][src-w3c-wcag22#contrast-minimum]
