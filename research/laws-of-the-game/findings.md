# Laws of the Game: findings

## Key concepts

- Current edition: Laws of the Game 2026/27, effective 1 July 2026, as of 2026-11-27; the 140th AGM approved the main changes (news 28 February 2026). [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#effective-date][src-ifab-news-140th-agm]
- A special meeting on 28 April 2026 added changes (circular 33); circular 34 (27 July 2026) clarified them; circular 33 is not in the printed booklet. [src-ifab-circular-33#effective][src-ifab-circular-34]
- Entry point is the IFAB documents hub; the bare /laws/latest/ address returned 404 on the research date. [src-ifab-laws-documents]
- The booklet holds Laws 1 to 17 then the VAR protocol; Law 11 is offside, Law 12 fouls and misconduct. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27]
- The referee's decisions on facts of play are final. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#referee-final]
- Other officials (assistant referees, fourth official, VAR, assistant VAR) assist; the referee decides. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#officials-list]
- Competitions may use technology for VAR, ball in or out, and semi-automated offside. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#saot-law5]
- Law 1 (field of play) belongs to pitch-geometry; touchlines run 90 m to 120 m. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#law1-touchline-length]
- PL 2026/27: the Football Principles keep a high threshold for VAR intervention. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-var-threshold]
- The FA hub summarises Laws 1 to 17 and links its Handbook. [src-fa-laws-and-rules]

## Must teach

- Law 3: at most eleven players including a goalkeeper; below seven a match cannot start or continue. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#min-seven-players]
- Law 3: official competitions allow up to five substitutes and three substitution opportunities. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#subs-five-three][src-ifab-laws-2026-27#sub-opportunities]
- Law 3 (2026/27): a substituted player must be off within 10 seconds of the board. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#sub-ten-seconds]
- If late, the substitute waits for the first stoppage after 1 minute. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#sub-protocol-one-minute]
- PL 2026/27: adopts the 10-second substitution exit. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-ten-second]
- Law 8: at kick-off opponents stay at least 9.15 m from the ball. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#kickoff-distance]
- Law 8: at a dropped ball other players stay at least 4 m away. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#dropped-ball-distance]
- Laws 9 and 10: the ball is out when wholly over a line; a goal needs the whole ball over the goal line, between posts and under the bar. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#ball-out-of-play][src-ifab-laws-2026-27#goal-scored]
- Law 12: contact fouls are direct free kicks. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#direct-free-kick-contact]
- Law 12: excessive force is a red card; reckless a yellow; careless no card. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#careless-reckless-force]
- Law 12: not every handball touch is an offence. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#handball-not-every-touch]
- Law 12: an arm making the body unnaturally bigger is a handball offence. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#handball-bigger]
- Law 12 (2026/27): no caution for a DOGSO foul when advantage is played and a goal follows. [src-ifab-law-changes-2026-27#dogso-advantage]
- PL 2026/27: not every contact is a foul; high threshold for penalising challenges. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-high-threshold-fouls]
- Law 13: opponents stay at least 9.15 m from a free kick. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#free-kick-distance]
- Law 13: against a wall of three or more, attackers stay at least 1 m away. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#free-kick-wall]
- Law 14: other players stay at least 9.15 m from the penalty mark. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#penalty-distance]
- Law 15: opponents stand at least 2 m from the throw-in point. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#throw-in-distance]
- Law 15: a throw-in into the opponents' goal gives a goal kick. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#throw-in-no-direct-goal]
- Laws 15 and 16 (2026/27): a five-second visual countdown applies to delayed throw-ins and goal kicks. [src-ifab-law-changes-2026-27#five-second-throw-in][src-ifab-law-changes-2026-27#five-second-goal-kick]
- A late throw-in goes to the opponents; a late goal kick gives them a corner. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#throw-in-countdown][src-ifab-laws-2026-27#goal-kick-countdown]
- The referee whistles, signals, then counts down from 5 seconds with a raised hand. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#countdown-protocol]
- PL 2026/27: adopts the five-second countdown. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-five-second]
- Law 17: opponents stay at least 9.15 m from the corner arc. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#corner-distance]
- Law 17: a keeper holding the ball over eight seconds concedes a corner. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#gk-eight-seconds]
- 2026/27: an injured or treated player leaves for 1 minute after the restart, with exceptions. [src-ifab-law-changes-2026-27#one-minute-off]
- PL 2026/27: at least 1 minute off, up from 30 seconds. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-one-minute]
- 2026/27: a team that causes a match to end below seven players forfeits, in principle. [src-ifab-circular-33#forfeit-seven]
- Offside position is not an offence. [src-ifab-offside#not-an-offence]
- Position: any part of head, body or feet in the opponents' half and nearer their goal line than both ball and second-last opponent. [src-ifab-offside#position-definition]
- Level with the second-last opponent, or the last two, is onside. [src-ifab-offside#level-is-onside]
- Hands and arms are ignored; the arm ends at the bottom of the armpit. [src-ifab-offside#hands-arms-ignored]
- Position is judged when a team-mate plays or touches the ball (first contact; last for a goalkeeper throw). [src-ifab-offside#first-contact]
- Offence needs active play: interfering with play, interfering with an opponent, or gaining an advantage. [src-ifab-offside#active-play]
- Advantage covers rebounds off posts, bar, officials or opponents, and deliberate saves. [src-ifab-offside#gaining-advantage]
- A deliberate play by an opponent, even handball, gives no advantage unless it was a save. [src-ifab-offside#deliberate-play]
- No offside offence from a goal kick, throw-in or corner. [src-ifab-offside#no-offence-restarts]
- Sanction: indirect free kick where the offence happened, even in the offender's half. [src-ifab-offside#indirect-free-kick]
- Assistant referees signal when an offside-position player may be penalised. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#assistant-referee-offside]
- PL practice (older page, verify): assistants may keep the flag down in tight offside calls with a goal chance. [src-pl-var-faqs#delayed-flag]
- Offside positions get a VAR-only review; interference is subjective, so an on-field review fits. [src-ifab-var-protocol#var-only-factual][src-ifab-var-protocol#ofr-subjective]
- PL 2026/27: semi-automated offside has run since 12 April 2025, and the Principles cite its improved use. [src-pl-saot-explainer#saot-start][src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-saot-principle]
- It suggests a kick point and draws lines on the second-last defender and attacker, using up to 30 cameras (several at 100 fps); the VAR checks. [src-pl-saot-explainer#saot-how][src-pl-saot-explainer#saot-cameras]
- VAR helps only on a clear and obvious error or serious missed incident, in five categories in 2026/27. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#var-categories][src-ifab-laws-2026-27#var-five-categories]
- Categories: goal, penalty, red card (including a clearly wrong second caution), mistaken identity, and corner as a competition option. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#var-corner-option]
- The original decision stands unless the review clearly shows a clear and obvious error. [src-ifab-var-protocol#clear-obvious-standard]
- The referee must decide first; only the referee starts a review, with no time limit. [src-ifab-var-protocol#no-no-decision][src-ifab-var-protocol#referee-initiates][src-ifab-var-protocol#no-time-limit]
- After a restart, review is limited to mistaken identity and violent conduct, spitting, biting or abuse. [src-ifab-var-protocol#after-restart]
- Only a clearly wrong second yellow is reviewable, not a missed one. [src-ifab-var-protocol#second-yellow-faq]
- PL 2026/27: adopts VAR review of a clearly wrong second yellow. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-second-yellow]
- A non-second yellow can be reviewed only to identify the player, not change the offence. [src-ifab-circular-34#mistaken-identity-limit]
- PL 2026/27: does not adopt mistaken-identity reversals of yellows for diving. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-mistaken-identity]
- PL 2026/27: does not adopt VAR review of incorrectly awarded corners. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-no-corner-review]
- IFAB makes the mouth-covering sending-off a competition option. [src-ifab-circular-33#mouth-option]
- PL 2026/27: does not adopt it; it uses a three-step discrimination protocol. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-mouth]
- PL 2026/27: keeps its own drinks-break protocol above 30 degrees Celsius at kick-off. [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-hydration]
- Trial: an outfield player leaves for 1 minute when the keeper needs treatment; the Premier League runs it. [src-ifab-circular-34#gk-injury-trial][src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-gk-trial]
- Trial: VAR may review an attacking offence before the ball is in play at a corner or attacking free kick. [src-ifab-circular-34#var-trial-set-piece]

## Misconceptions

- "Level is offside": level with the second-last opponent is onside. [src-ifab-offside#level-is-onside]
- "Being in an offside position is a foul": it is not an offence. [src-ifab-offside#not-an-offence]
- "Offside applies to every pass": not from a goal kick, throw-in or corner. [src-ifab-offside#no-offence-restarts]
- "An arm can put you offside": hands and arms are ignored. [src-ifab-offside#hands-arms-ignored]
- "Handball is any touch": not every touch is an offence. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#handball-not-every-touch]
- "A throw-in can score directly": a goal kick follows instead. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#throw-in-no-direct-goal]
- "Players or managers can call for VAR": they cannot. [src-ifab-var-protocol#players-no-request]
- "VAR can add a missed second yellow": it cannot. [src-ifab-var-protocol#second-yellow-faq]

## Contested points

Contested: Does VAR improve decisions? The Premier League panel reports correct key decisions rose from 82 per cent to 96 per cent, while a 9,076-match meta-analysis found no meaningful change in goals per match; the League concedes reviews slow the flow. [src-pl-var-accuracy-2024#accuracy-82-96][src-rogerson-var-meta-analysis#no-goal-effect][src-rogerson-var-meta-analysis#sample][src-pl-var-accuracy-2024#too-slow]

Contested: Is on-field review a safeguard or a formality? IFAB sends subjective calls to the review area, yet a study of 1520 Premier League matches found 250 monitor visits and about 95.1% overturned. [src-ifab-var-protocol#ofr-subjective][src-walker-var-review-plos-2026#overturn-rate][src-walker-var-review-plos-2026#overturned-share]

Contested: Should offside become "daylight" offside? The Canadian trial counts a player onside if any scoring body part is level with the second-last defender; reported results were 54% fewer offside decisions and about 13 more goals in 112 matches, against defensive lines dropping three metres and IFAB panels cooling (one trade report). [src-fifa-cpl-daylight-offside-trial#daylight-rule][src-insideworldfootball-wenger-offside-trial#trial-results][src-insideworldfootball-wenger-offside-trial#defensive-line][src-insideworldfootball-wenger-offside-trial#ifab-cool]

Contested: Should VAR widen to corners? IFAB permits corner review as a competition option; the Premier League declines to enlarge VAR's scope. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#var-corner-option][src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-no-corner-review]

Contested: Can mistaken identity police simulation? IFAB called the World Cup use well received but barred it until a review ends; the Premier League does not adopt it. [src-ifab-circular-34#mistaken-identity-world-cup][src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-mistaken-identity]
