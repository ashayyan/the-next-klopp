# Visual ideas: Laws of the Game

## Draggable offside line

Lesson Loop step: Touch, then Predict. The learner drags an attacker along a pitch strip while a second-last defender and the ball stay fixed. The offside line is a thin rule through the second-last opponent. Level shows as green (onside), ahead as amber, and the arm or hand zone is greyed to show it never counts. The learner predicts before releasing: offside or not? The line also turns off when the pass comes from a throw-in, goal kick or corner. [src-ifab-offside#position-definition] [src-ifab-offside#level-is-onside] [src-ifab-offside#no-offence-restarts]

## Position versus offence timeline

Lesson Loop step: See. A scrubber shows the moment the ball is played; a ghost freezes every player there. After the freeze, the learner toggles three outcomes: no involvement (no offence), interfering with play, interfering with an opponent. A fourth toggle covers the rebound or save case. [src-ifab-offside#active-play] [src-ifab-offside#gaining-advantage] [src-ifab-offside#deliberate-play]

## VAR decision path

Lesson Loop step: Do. A branching strip starts at the referee's call, then asks: is it one of five categories, and is the error clear and obvious? The learner places incidents (a missed second yellow, a tight offside, a corner) into the path. A Premier League toggle switches off the corner branch. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#var-five-categories] [src-ifab-var-protocol#clear-obvious-standard] [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-no-corner-review]

## Restart picker

Lesson Loop step: Predict. A pitch shows the ball crossing a line after a touch from either side; the learner picks throw-in, goal kick or corner, then sees the restart that follows. A challenge shows a goal scored straight from a throw-in. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#throw-in-no-direct-goal] [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#goal-kick-countdown]

## Five-second countdown stopwatch

Lesson Loop step: Hook, then Lock in. The learner plays a time-wasting goalkeeper; a raised-hand countdown runs from 5 and the restart flips to the opponents. A second pass shows the substitution exit clock: 10 seconds, then a one-minute wait. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#countdown-protocol] [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#sub-ten-seconds]

## Edition and Premier League badge

Lesson Loop step: Connect. Every rule card carries the edition tag (2026/27, effective 1 July 2026) and a PL badge when the League differs from IFAB, such as injury time off or corner review. A semi-automated offside panel draws the kick-point and lines. [src-ifab-laws-2026-27#effective-date] [src-pl-saot-explainer#saot-how] [src-pl-laws-2026-27#pl-one-minute]
