## Data credit badge (Show step)
Every chart carries a small credit strip: provider name, licence label, link to the licence deed. CC BY sources show the provider text; StatsBomb charts show the StatsBomb logo slot required by the agreement [src-statsbomb-open-data#agreement-1-4-logo-credit]. The strip sits under the figure so it never covers the pitch, and keyboard users can focus its link. CC BY credit means naming the source, linking the licence and noting changes [src-creativecommons-by-4-0#cc-by-attribution].

## Coverage heat grid (Show step)
A grid of providers by data type, cells shaded by Premier League match count, read from coverage-matrix.json. The learner sees at a glance that StatsBomb 2015/16 and Wyscout 2017/18 hold the events, while 360 and physical columns stay empty for the Premier League [src-statsbomb-open-data][src-wyscout-public-dataset]. A hover card on each cell gives the licence verdict.

## Can I ship this? checker (Touch step)
The learner picks a provider and an action (read, fetch, ship, derive) and a four-light panel answers yes, no, unknown or conditional, with the clause that decides it. Wrong guesses reveal the clause, such as the no-third-party rule on StatsBomb [src-statsbomb-open-data#agreement-1-2-1-no-third-party].

## Licence sorting game (Practise step)
Drag provider cards into three bins: ships with credit, local only, never. Feedback cites the source. Includes the trap cases: a CC0 repository built from a site with its own terms [src-transfermarkt-datasets][src-transfermarkt-site], and an MIT file on a data repository [src-skillcorner-opendata].

## Terms timeline (Show step)
A short timeline marks FBref's advanced stats removal on 2026-01-20 and the pause of transfermarkt-datasets in July 2026, showing why registry entries carry an as-of date [src-awfulannouncing-fbref-removal][src-transfermarkt-datasets#readme-updates-paused].

## Embed rule demo (Touch step)
A mock video frame where dragging a label over the player turns the frame red with the overlay rule, and moving it beside the player turns it green. Teaches why Watch Gym puts notes next to the player, not on it [src-youtube-api-rmf#rmf-no-overlays].
