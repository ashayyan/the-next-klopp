## One goal, many small decisions

Manchester City scored against Liverpool after the ball moved through a chain of City players. Every badge on the pitch marks one of their actions.

</Step>

<Step id="see-the-chain">

## Watch the chain from start to finish

The move starts with Mangala in his own half and ends with Leroy Sané's finish. Follow the ball as each player passes it on.

</Step>

## Sané finishes the move

Leroy Sané takes the shot and scores. The chain ends where the ball crosses the goal line.

<Step id="do-the-goal-rule">

## When does a goal count?

A goal counts only when the whole ball crosses the goal line between the posts and under the bar. Check the rule against the finish.

</Step>
